A big, simple phone app for older people

Large buttons, large letters, and one tap to answer. Made for older people and anyone who finds small screens hard to read.

Get it on Google Play

What it does

  • Huge incoming call screen – tap the big green area to answer, hold the red area to decline.
  • Says who is calling – “Anna is calling”, read aloud.
  • Big favourites with photos – one tap calls.
  • Contacts with voice search – say a name to find it.
  • Big keypad and recent calls in plain colours; a missed-call notification with the number of missed calls on the app icon.
  • Emergency button (112 or 911, depending on your SIM card’s country). It can be hidden in Settings.
  • Strong vibration and flashlight alert for incoming calls.
  • For low vision, hearing loss and shaky hands – yellow-on-black and dark colours, keys read aloud, an optional 3-second countdown before each call, a buzz when the other person answers or hangs up, decline with a ready-made text message, a shaky-hands setting and a practice call.
  • 18 languages: English, Turkish, Spanish, German, French, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Hindi, Bengali, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional).

Your contacts stay on your phone. The app does not upload your contacts, call history or phone numbers anywhere. It has no servers of its own.

The app shows one small advertisement for a short time – at most 30 seconds, and again only after the app has been in the background for a while – at the bottom of the favourites, recent calls and contacts lists; never on the call, contact card, emergency or settings screens. The ad is provided by Google, which collects some technical device data for it. It can be removed with an optional one-time purchase through Google Play. Read the privacy policy.

Good to know

  • Needs Android 10 or newer.
  • The app becomes your phone’s default phone app. You can switch back to the original one at any time – see how.
  • It handles normal mobile network calls. WhatsApp and other internet calls keep their own screens.

Türkçe

Yaşlılar ve az görenler için büyük, sade telefon uygulaması.

  • Dev gelen arama ekranı: yeşile dokun yanıtla, kırmızıya basılı tut reddet.
  • Arayanın adını sesli okur, büyük favoriler, sesle kişi arama, büyük tuş takımı.
  • Acil düğmesi (SIM kartın ülkesine göre 112 veya 911).
  • Az görme, işitme kaybı ve titreyen eller için: sarı-siyah ve koyu renkler, tuşları sesli okuma, isteğe bağlı aramadan önce 3 saniye bekleme, karşı taraf açınca/kapatınca titreşim, hazır mesajla reddetme, titreyen el ayarı ve alıştırma araması.
  • Uygulama rehberinizi ve arama geçmişinizi internete yüklemez.
  • Cevapsız arama bildirimi; cevapsız arama sayısı uygulama simgesinde görünür.
  • Favoriler, son aramalar ve rehber listelerinin altında, en çok 30 saniye (uygulama bir süre arka planda kaldıktan sonra yeniden) görünen tek küçük reklam bandı vardır (Google); arama, kişi kartı, acil ve ayarlar ekranlarında asla reklam yoktur. Reklam, isteğe bağlı tek seferlik bir satın almayla (Google Play) kaldırılabilir.
